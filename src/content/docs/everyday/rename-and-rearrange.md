---
title: Rename and rearrange
description: Rename and reorder what is in an Archive without renaming files yourself.
---

Names and order change as an Archive grows. You can change them without renaming files yourself.

## Rename

Double-click a title in the manuscript to rename it in place. Enter keeps the name. Escape puts the old name back. You can also rename the selected item from the Manuscript menu.

The new name is the name you see. The piece is still the same piece. A person you rename in Lore is still the same record. You do not have to edit filenames to make the new name stick, and the connections Studio keeps do not drop because the title changed.

To rename the Archive itself, use the File menu while it is open. The window title uses that name the next time you look for it.

## Rearrange

Drag a piece to change its order, or move it under another valid parent. Drop it between its neighbors to reorder. An invalid drop is refused, so a piece is not forced into a place it cannot occupy.

Move Up and Move Down are on the context menu when that direction is possible.

Reordering does not rename the files, and it does not make you rebuild the surrounding material. A note that pointed at a piece still points at it after you move the piece.

For a novel, what may sit under what — and how to set a stretch aside or remove it — is covered in [Organize a novel](/everyday/organize-a-novel/). Scrapbook is the place to park material. Recycle is how you remove it, with the files kept until you empty that folder yourself.
