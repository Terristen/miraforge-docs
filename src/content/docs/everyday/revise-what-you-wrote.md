---
title: Revise what you wrote
description: Change writing that is already in the Archive. In a novel, that prose is in a scene.
---

Open the writing that is already in the Archive and change it there. The copy you mean to keep is the one in the Archive, not a second file you maintain beside it.

## Change the open piece

1. Open the piece you want to revise.
2. Change the words in the editor.
3. Use Edit → Find (Ctrl+F) and Edit → Replace (Ctrl+H) when you are hunting a phrase in the piece you have open. Those commands stay in the editor. They do not search the rest of the Archive.
4. Use Format, or the Style group on the ribbon, for emphasis and links. Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, and Ctrl+K are the same shortcuts as in a first session.
5. Save from the ribbon when this revision should be the copy stored in the Archive.

## If this Archive is a novel

In a novel, the prose is in a Scene. Open the scene and edit it there.

The chapter view only assembles that chapter’s scenes so you can read them in order. It does not hold a separate copy of the prose, and you do not revise in it. Change the scene. The next time you open the chapter, the view shows what the scene says now.
