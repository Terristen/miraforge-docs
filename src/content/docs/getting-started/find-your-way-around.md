An open Archive fills the window. These are the parts you will use in a first session.

## The window

**Manuscript** is the shape of the writing, listed down one side.

**Lore** is the material you keep beside the writing, grouped by type. The types belong to this Archive. One Archive might group people, places, and organizations. Another might use different names. The names are yours. They are not a fixed list Studio imposes.

**The editor** is the center, where you write.

**Metadata** is the facts about whatever you have selected, starting with its title. The fields change with the selection.

**Dossier** is a reading of the lore that applies where you are in the work.

**Archive Files** is the folder itself: the files Studio is keeping for this Archive.

## Show and hide

The menu bar is File, Edit, Manuscript, Format, View, Tools, and Help.

The View menu shows or hides Lore, the inspector (the Metadata panel), the Dossier, and Archive Files. Hide a panel when you want the editor to have the room. Show it when you need it again.

The ribbon under the menu follows what you are doing. Find and Lore sit toward the left. When the editor is open, Style sits at the right: emphasis, lists, and links.
